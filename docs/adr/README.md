# Architecture decision records

ADR-0001 through ADR-0010 are each traceable to a finding in `docs/audit/2026-08-29-council-review-v2.md`, a 9-lens adversarial review that returned **BLOCKED for implementation** on the predecessor design with 11 confirmed critical findings. ADR-0011 onward were written later, and each cites the audit, review or measurement that forced it; the last column of the table names it. The applied design is `docs/design/v3.md`.

That provenance is the point. An ADR that says "we chose X because it is better" is a rationalization. An ADR that says "we chose X because five lenses independently found that Y fails in this specific way, and here is the measurement" is a record. Each document below cites the finding that forced it, states the consequences that are _bad_ as well as the ones that are good, and says why each rejected alternative was rejected.

## Notes on reading these records

**Decision and implementation are not the same thing.** Several records decide a mechanism that release 0.1.0 (2026-11-10) does not contain, for example the Postgres adapter in ADR-0004 and the annotations table in ADR-0007. Each such record carries a dated "Status of implementation" note near its Status line that says what exists, what does not, and where the code is. The decision text is left as accepted.

**"An earlier internal project".** ADR-0001, 0002, 0003, 0004, 0007 and 0010 cite an earlier internal project as corroboration. That project is not published, so a reader cannot check those sentences. Treat them as context. Each of those decisions is argued in its own record from the council findings, which are in this repository.

**ADR-0017.** [ADR-0017](ADR-0017-validated-write-contract-identity.md) permits a path-free natural key for the local Zod write-binding producer. The bounded producer and its historical parent/fix proof are recorded there. Hardened extraction remains UNKNOWN and dormant on the measured development corpus; current implementation acceptance and independent qualification remain pending.

**Moved paths.** The Redactor that [ADR-0011](ADR-0011-redactor-wired-as-sole-writer.md), [ADR-0013](ADR-0013-redactor-never-rewrites-identity.md) and [ADR-0015](ADR-0015-semantic-engine-optional-profile-layer.md) cite as `packages/core/src/security/redactor.ts` (and its sibling modules `patterns.ts`, `entropy.ts`, `untrusted.ts`) now lives in `packages/security/src/` as `@seamline/security`. `packages/core/src/security/redacting-store.ts` stays in core. Those records keep their original text; their decisions are unchanged.

**The semantic engine.** The additive semantic engine has its own implementation council and [ADR-0012](ADR-0012-extensible-semantic-foundation.md); it preserves the original analysis contracts. [ADR-0015](ADR-0015-semantic-engine-optional-profile-layer.md) records the boundary that keeps it an optional layer, with tests for its structural claims and a list of what they do not cover.

**ADR-0002 and ADR-0009.** The rename gate they state and ADR-0002's statement that `path` is never part of identity are amended for `File` by [ADR-0016](ADR-0016-file-identity-carries-its-path.md), which also states the inputs whose change under a move or rename changes another kind's id. The gate as shipped is stated in `docs/design/v3.md` §2.1.

## Reading order

If you are new to the project, ADR-0005 is the one that explains what Seamline is _for_. ADR-0001 through 0004 are the machinery that makes it work, and 0016, 0017 and 0018 are later corrections to that machinery (identity for `File`, identity for one contract producer, and narrower deletion authority), best read after 0002 and 0003. 0006, 0007, 0009, 0011, and 0013 are the constraints that keep it honest. 0008 and 0010 are how it ships. 0014 adds the alignment report (`seamline align`) beside the contradiction pipeline and is best read after 0005. 0012 and 0015 concern the optional semantic layer and can be skipped by a reader who only uses the contradiction pipeline.

## Format

Status, Date, Context, Decision, Consequences (positive **and** negative), Alternatives considered (each with a reason for rejection). A consequences section listing only benefits means the decision was not actually examined. ADR-0012, ADR-0017 and ADR-0018 use a shorter layout: 0017 and 0018 state the forcing problem in the header or under "Problem" in place of Context, and all three state costs in a single Consequences or Alternatives section.

## Amending an ADR

These records are immutable once accepted. A decision that changes gets a **new** ADR that supersedes the old one, and the old one is marked `Superseded by ADR-NNNN` with its content left intact. The history of a decision is part of the decision.

A decision that is narrowed or corrected without being replaced gets a new ADR that says `Amends ADR-NNNN` in its Status line, and the old record gets only a note appended to its Status line, `Amended by ADR-NNNN` followed by a clause saying what changed, with the rest of its text left intact ([ADR-0011](ADR-0011-redactor-wired-as-sole-writer.md) and [ADR-0002](ADR-0002-path-free-natural-key-identity.md) are marked this way).

A narrower change, one that leaves the decision standing and moves only the edge of its scope, may instead be recorded as an amendment: the new ADR states the scope of the change, and the old record gains a one-line `Amended by ADR-NNNN` note and is otherwise untouched. ADR-0013 amends ADR-0011 this way, and ADR-0014 amends ADR-0005.

A record whose decision stands but whose implementation differs from its text gets a dated `Status of implementation` note in its header, as described above. A record that adds to what an earlier one defines without changing its decision is marked `Extends ADR-NNNN`, and the earlier record gains an `Extended by ADR-NNNN` clause on its Status line ([ADR-0018](ADR-0018-canonical-name-authority.md) extends ADR-0003 and ADR-0009 this way). Neither kind of note changes the decision text.
